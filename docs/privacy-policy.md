# Privacy Policy for Navica Keys

Effective: 2026-11-12

Published at https://navica.llc/tools/navica-keys/privacy/. Keep this file and that page in step.

Navica Keys is a Chrome extension published by Navica LLC. It adds keyboard
shortcuts and on-screen key hints to the Triage inbox in Karbon's web app. This policy explains
what it handles. The short version: it collects nothing, and it sends nothing to us.

## Where it runs

The extension runs only on Karbon's web app (app2.karbonhq.com, and its staging site webapp-stage.karbonhq.com). It can't see any other site.

## What the extension reads

- **Karbon's buttons, to press them for you.** When you press a shortcut, the extension finds
  Karbon's matching button on the page (such as Clear or Add to My Week) and presses it, the
  same as a click. It also finds those buttons to place the key hints next to them.
- **Which email is open.** It checks which email in the list is open, so a shortcut acts on
  the right one.

It doesn't read, copy, or keep the content of your emails, clients, or work items. Karbon
carries out each action itself, through your own signed-in Karbon session. The extension makes
no requests of its own to Karbon or anywhere else.

## What the extension stores

- **Your shortcut settings:** your keys, which shortcuts and hints are on, hint style, and the
  My Week target. These are kept in Chrome's sync storage, so if you have turned on Chrome
  sync, Google syncs them between your own browsers.

Removing the extension deletes them.

## What we never do

- We don't collect, sell, or share any data. The extension has no servers, no account, and no
  analytics.
- It keeps no record of your emails or of what you do in Karbon.
- It loads no code from outside the extension.
